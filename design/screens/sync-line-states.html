SYNC VIEW · LINE MODEReading the gap in millimetres
User, 8 Nov. On every tick a white line leaves the start mark and moves down at exactly 0.2 mm per ms, whatever the phone’s size or density. Line two phones up by their start marks: the gap between the lines is the time gap, 2 mm for every 10 ms. 100 mm of travel covers 500 ms, then the line waits for the next tick.
Two phones side by sideR’s line is 3 mm higher: R is 15 ms behind. The phone that’s behind is always the one whose line is higher. Phones needn’t be the same size; only the start marks need to meet.
Why 0.2 mm per msFast enough that 10 ms is easy to see (2 mm), slow enough that 100 mm (most phone screens) holds 500 ms, longer than any real gap. At 120 Hz the line steps 1.7 mm a frame; at 60 Hz 3.3 mm. A faint trail shows the last three frames so steps don’t read as gaps.
Each beat or each secondBeat: starts on every beat, so the sweep follows the music (124 BPM leaves 484 ms, about 97 mm). Second: starts on every flowtime second, for when nothing’s playing or the beat isn’t steady.

CARD CHECK · FIRST TIME, AND FROM CONTROLS
Check the ruler with a cardPhones report their screen density, and some get it slightly wrong, which would make 2 mm not 2 mm. Lay any bank or ID card along the length of the screen, its end on the start mark, and drag the end mark to the card’s far edge. Shown sideways here.
