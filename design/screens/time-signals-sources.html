WHAT EACH NODE MEANS
FlowtimeThe shared clock every Brocast phone plays to: UK time, Teddington · Greenwich. It always follows the most accurate source below. Details: source in charge, error, hand-overs this session.
GPSTime from navigation satellites, read from this phone’s GPS chip. The best a phone can get, usually well under a millisecond. Details: satellites used of seen, Gold or Silver link, last fix, error; links to Satellites.
HoldThis phone’s own clock carrying on from the last satellite fix. Details: error now, how fast it grows, last fix, when internet time would take over.
Internet time server (NTP)A time server reached over the internet. S1 means one step from an atomic clock; NPL is the UK’s National Physical Laboratory. Details: server, stratum, its reference, round trip, difference from GPS, error, in use or discarded.
