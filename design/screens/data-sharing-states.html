DATA AND SHARINGProfiles, Auto’s reasons, network changes
WHAT LIGHT DOES
Downloads in short bursts: tops up to 15 min ahead whenever it drops under 2 min, then lets the radio sleep. Kindest to the battery.
On Wi-Fi: shares with other listeners only while charging, up to 1 Mbps.
On mobile data: downloads up to 100 MB a day. Never shares.
Up to 4 connections and 24 MB of memory; holds up to 1 GB to share. Finds peers but doesn’t announce itself.
Timing is never affected: every profile plays in perfect sync.
WHAT GENEROUS DOES
Keeps a full day ready: tops up to 24 h ahead whenever it drops under 30 min.
On Wi-Fi: always shares, at any battery level, with no speed limit.
On mobile data: downloads without a daily limit. Still never shares unless you allow it.
Up to 24 connections and 192 MB of memory; holds up to 10 GB to share. Always announces itself.
Timing is never affected: every profile plays in perfect sync.
AUTO · WHY IT CHOSE
MESH · LIGHT · battery 18%Under 20% and not charging: saves battery first.
MESH · LIGHT · phone warmRunning hot: fewer connections and longer sleeps until it cools.
MESH · BALANCED · Wi-FiOn Wi-Fi with a healthy battery: the default.
MESH · GENEROUS · chargingOn Wi-Fi and charging: shares freely, keeps a full day ready.
MESH · DATA SAVERAndroid Data Saver is on: no sharing on mobile data, downloads only as needed.
The reason shows under the profile buttons and in the event log each time Auto changes profile.
MOVING BETWEEN NETWORKS
Moved to mobile data: sharing stopped. Downloads carry on, up to 380 MB more today.
The moment the phone joins a metered network (mobile data, a hotspot or paid Wi-Fi), uploads and announcing stop and it keeps only the connections it needs to download. Back on Wi-Fi, sharing resumes by itself. Every byte is counted against the network it used.
