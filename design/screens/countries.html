Show your country to other listeners?
Phones that connect to yours already see your internet address. With this on, they may look up which country it’s in and show it next to your name, like “Canada”. Nothing more precise: no city, no position, and your GPS never leaves this phone. With a VPN, they’ll see the VPN’s country.Off: you still see other listeners’ countries if they’ve allowed it, and you’re still counted as a listener.

RULES
Who shows upA phone’s country shows only if that phone has Show my country on. Its announcement carries the permission; every phone honours it before looking anything up.
Where the lookup happensOn this phone, from a country table that ships with the app and updates over the mesh like the engine. No address is ever sent to an outside service.
Relayed and unknownA relayed listener’s address is the relay’s, so its country is unknown, never guessed. Shown as a count, not a flag.
Only connected listenersAddresses are known only for phones you’re connected to, so the card says “of the 15 listeners this phone is connected to”, never a station-wide claim.
