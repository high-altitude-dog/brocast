Show your headphones’ name?
Brocast can show which headphones are playing, like “Sony WH-1000XM5”, and remember a separate lag for each pair. Android calls this permission Nearby devices. Brocast only reads the name and sound settings of headphones already connected to this phone. It doesn’t look for other devices, and it isn’t used to find other listeners.
