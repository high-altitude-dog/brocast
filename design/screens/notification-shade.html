WHAT THE APP SUPPLIES Title: the track, or the set or show when there's no track name. Subtitle: artist and set, for example Mara Okoye · Golden Hour Set. Both switch when the new track reaches the ear, not when its name arrives. Artwork: the sync state tile, so music takes the words and sync takes the picture. The badge text also goes in the album slot, for car and watch displays. Left button: Use satellites, as a custom action whose icon shows on or off. One tap from anywhere, even when locked. Main button: Stop and Play. Play rejoins the live point at D; there's no pause on a shared timeline. No seek bar: the session sets no duration, because it's live. Tapping the card opens Now playing.
