CAMERA TEST CARD · RULESWhat changes, and when
Between flashesPure black. Label, flowtime, code and corners stay exactly where they are. Flowtime is shown to the millisecond but only changes at each flash, to the flash’s own time, so nothing moves between flashes and the camera only sees one change per flash.
The flashThe whole screen white for 2 display frames (about 33 ms at 60 Hz, 17 ms at 120 Hz), with the label, time and code in black. Timed to the display’s presentation time for the beat, not when it was drawn.
The codeTwo reference blocks (on, off) to set the threshold, then 8 bits: the flowtime second mod 256, most significant first. With several flashes a second, beats are told apart by the beat label and their spacing.
The clickA 10 ms tone burst at flowtime plus the output’s lag, so it reaches the ear on the beat. Left 2 kHz, Right 3 kHz, Stereo one in each ear. The filming phone’s microphone can tell phones apart by pitch.
Never more than 3 a secondAbove 180 BPM, Every beat flashes on every other beat, keeping under the usual limit for photosensitive viewers.
No beatFlash on falls back to every flowtime second; the beat label reads `NO BEAT · SECOND`.
LeavingHold anywhere for 2 s; a ring fills under the finger. Taps do nothing, so a stray touch can’t change the card mid-test.
If sync is lostThe card keeps flashing but the corner shows `OUT OF SYNC ±41 ms` in amber, so the video carries the warning too.
