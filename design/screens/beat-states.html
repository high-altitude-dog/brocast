WHY IT’S HEREThe beats flip at the moment the sound reaches your ears (flowtime plus your headphones’ delay), not when the phone decodes it. So every phone in the crowd lights beat 1 together: a quick way to see the sync with your own eyes. Tap it to open Buffer, where the same beats sit under the waveform.
